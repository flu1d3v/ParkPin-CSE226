\# Privacy Policy for Wayback



Last updated: November 7, 2026



Wayback ("the app") saves the location of your parked vehicle so you can find it later.



\## What the app uses

\- \*\*Location:\*\* read only when you tap Save, to record where you parked. It is stored on your device.

\- \*\*Photos:\*\* if you choose to add a photo, it is taken with your device's camera app and stored in the app's private storage on your device.

\- \*\*Photo labels:\*\* produced on your device by Google ML Kit's on-device image labeling.



\## What the developer collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no developer-run servers. Your saved spots, photos and labels are not sent to the developer or to any third party by the app.



Google ML Kit and Google Play services are libraries from Google that run inside the app. Their own handling of diagnostic information is described by Google at developers.google.com/ml-kit and policies.google.com/privacy.



\## Your control

You can delete any saved spot, together with its photo, from the History screen. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.



\## Children

The app is not directed at children.
